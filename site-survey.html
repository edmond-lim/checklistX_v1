Cap bank check — procedure
  1. Locate the capacitor bank panel, beside or inside the existing MSB.
  2. Read PF off the controller display.
  3. Count contactor / step lamps — total installed.
  4. Note steps reading normal against steps in fault or trip.
  5. Confirm regulator fitted, display live not frozen.
  6. Photograph the controller display, legible.
  7. Flag if PF < 0.85, any step in fault, or no PFC panel despite past low-PF charges.

0/1
LMRA — procedure
  1. Stop at the work position before stepping onto the roof or opening any panel.
  2. Scan: roof edges, skylights and fragile sheets, wet or mossy surfaces, exposed live parts, weather, blocked access.
  3. Confirm PPE and access — harness clipped to anchor point at height, footwear, second person aware.
  4. If unsafe, correct it first: wait out rain, isolate the MSB, get a second person.
  5. Record one row per hazard. If none, write “No hazard identified — proceed.”
  6. Repeat after any break, weather change, or move to a new work area.
