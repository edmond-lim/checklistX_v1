MSB & Roof Markup

On a satellite view of the site, mark where the main switchboard (MSB) is, which roofs can take solar panels, and how a cable could run between them. When you are done, save the marked image and send it back on WhatsApp.

What to mark

Each marker has its own colour so the photos are easy to read later.

  • MSBTap on the switchboard room or its door.
  • Panel areaDraw around the roof that can take panels.
  • Cable routeDraw the path from the MSB to the panel area.
  • ✕
    AvoidTap on tall trees, water tanks, skylights, aircon units, weak roof.
  • 1
    NoteTap a spot, then type what it is below.

1. Add your satellite screenshot

Open Google Maps or Google Earth, switch to Satellite, zoom in until the whole building and the MSB room fill the screen, then take a screenshot. You can add more than one.

Showing an example image

2. Mark the photo

Pick a marker, then tap or draw on the photo.

Example satellite view

3. Add details

Notes are printed under the photo when you save it.

No numbered notes on this photo yet. Use the Note marker to add one.

4. Save and send

Makes one marked image per photo, with your notes underneath.

To send: tap Share under a marked photo and pick WhatsApp. If Share isn't offered, press and hold the photo, choose Save image or Add to Photos, then send it. On a computer, use Download image.

Photos stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded until you send the saved image yourself.
