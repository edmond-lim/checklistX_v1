Capacity check

Non-Domestic: inverter output must be ≤100% of MD, and ≤1,000 kW overall. Domestic: 5 kW single phase / 15 kW three phase before CCC applies.

Technical assessment study

Prerequisite for approval — determined by voltage level and capacity tier.

Monthly export credit estimate

MAQ = capacity (kWac) × 5 sun hours × billing days. Export offset is capped at the lower of MAQ or monthly import — excess is forfeited, unused export is not carried forward.

Structural differences confirmed in the ATAP guideline: Non-Domestic export is credited at Average SMP (not a fixed tariff rate), export offset is capped at MAQ and at the same billing period's import, unused export forfeits with no carry-forward, and credit cannot offset the Automatic Fuel Adjustment. NEM NOVA's own crediting mechanism sits in a separate guideline — confirm current rates and rollover rules there before quoting a client.
The guideline only states duration for a handful of steps (bold below). Everything else — study turnaround, physical install, testing — is project-dependent and not fixed by the guideline. The 18-month figure is a maximum deadline from approval to commissioning, not an expected installation duration. Edit the fields below to model your own project.

Phase A · Pre-approval (duration is yours to plan)

Runs from RPVSP appointment to SEDA's e-Certificate. No fixed floor — driven by how fast your consultant delivers the study and how fast you assemble documents.

Phase B · Post-approval — build to commissioning

Statutory ceiling: 18 months from the date of SEDA's approval notification (§15.8). Miss it and the approval is auto-cancelled with fees forfeited. Installation, T&C, and admin steps below are editable estimates — set them to your actual EPC programme.
